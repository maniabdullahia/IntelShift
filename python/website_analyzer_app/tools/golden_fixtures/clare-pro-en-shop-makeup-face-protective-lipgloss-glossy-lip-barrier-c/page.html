  • DECRIPTION

    +

    GLOSSY LIP BARRIER is an innovative product intended for deep lip care. It creates a juicy, glassy surface on the lips with a long-lasting mirror shine. Its honey-like consistency covers the skin of the lips with a transparent, rich-feeling coating that stays on them for long hours, providing a feeling of deep hydration and comfort. The product is based on a specially selected composition of emollients with excellent occlusive properties, which translate into a strong protective effect for the exceptionally delicate and thin epidermis of the red lip. The basic ingredients of GLOSSY LIP BARRIER have excellent adhesion and a unique long-lasting effect without excessive stickiness, and also have an excellent water-binding ability, which significantly affects the skin's hydration.
    The natural, cold-pressed oils contained in GLOSSY LIP BARRIER have been specially selected to support the barrier functions of the epidermis. Meadowfoam oil, raspberry seed oil, Arctic cranberry oil, blueberry seed oil, sunflower oil and squalane are not a coincidental combination - rich in essential fatty acids, antioxidants and vitamins, whose main task is to protect and regenerate the thin, delicate skin of the lips, which does not have any sebaceous glands and it is necessary to provide it with an additional barrier and support in contact with the external environment. Selected oils guarantee the skin nourishment, softness, deep hydration and regeneration.

  • HOW TO USE

    +

    To maintain the effect of beautiful and healthy lips, you should use protective lip gloss regularly, at least 3 times a day - especially in winter or summer, when your lips are exposed to frost, wind or sun.

  • INGREDIENTS

    +

    GLOSSY LIP BARRIER contains as many as 3 technologically advanced active ingredients with proven lip-filling properties - advanced and synergistic peptides: VOLULIP™, MAXILIP™ and LINEFILL™ mustard extract. Similarly to all CLARE PRO care products, GLOSSY LIP BARRIER also uses maximum concentrations of active substances that guarantee visible effects. The ingredients used in the formulation have proven effects - they moisturize, nourish and regenerate the skin of the red lip. They also help fill wrinkles through the synthesis of collagen and elastin, and nourish fat cells inside the skin to increase the volume and firmness of the lips by lifting them from the inside. This extremely rich combination of active ingredients makes the lips smooth and soft with regular use, and their contour is better defined.
    The product consists of 96% ingredients of natural origin.
    Unlike most lip glosses on the market, it is not based on petroleum derivatives, but on natural emollients.

    INCI: POLYGLYCERYL-2 ISOSTEARATE/DIMER DILINOLEATE COPOLYMER, CAPRYLIC/CAPRIC TRIGLYCERIDE, SQUALANE, DIMETHYL ISOSORBIDE, LIMNANTHES ALBA SEED OIL, RUBUS IDAEUS SEED OIL, VACCINIUM CORYMBOSUM SEED OIL, OXYCOCCUS PALUSTRIS SEED OIL, HELIANTHUS ANNUUS SEED OIL, PORTULACA PILOSA EXTRACT, SESAMUM INDICUM SEED EXTRACT, PALMITOYL TRIPEPTIDE-38, PALMITOYL TRIPEPTIDE-1, TOCOPHEROL, SUCROSE COCOATE, CETEARYL ETHYLHEXANOATE, SORBITAN ISOSTEARATE, ETHYLHEXYL PALMITATE, TRIBEHENIN, POLYGLYCERYL-2 TRIISOSTEARATE, AROMA, [+/-: CI 77891, CI 15850, CI 77491, CI 15985, CI 77499, CI 77007, CI 19140, CI 45410].

  • PRODUCT DNA

    +

    An innovative emollient of natural origin. Thanks to its honey-like, rich consistency, it gives the product excellent adhesion to the skin and a unique long-lasting effect without excessive stickiness. It binds water perfectly and coats the lips with a durable, mirror-gloss film.

    Volulip™ is an active substance containing a plant extract and a natural modified peptide imitating matrikine, which fills the lips through the synthesis of hyaluronic acid. It improves the internal and superficial texture of the lips with a visible impact on their shape. The product increases the volume, hydration and softness of the lips. It has anti-aging and anti-wrinkle properties. Used in the recommended concentration for which the tests were performed - 1%.

    Maxi-Lip™ is a natural peptide stabilized by combining with a palmitoyl chain. Stimulates the synthesis of collagen and glycosaminoglycans, especially hyaluronic acid, to make the lips better moisturized, better defined, firmer and smoother. This product is used in lip care and makeup.
    Used in the recommended concentration for which the tests were performed - 1%.

    Linefill™ is an active ingredient of plant origin that is able to increase the accumulation of lipids in fatty tissue, giving greater volume to facial areas that lose their shape due to aging. Thanks to its action, Linefill™ visibly fills wrinkles and recesses of the skin, and also gives volume to the lips, resulting in a rejuvenated, relaxed and attractive face. Used in the recommended concentration for which the tests were performed - 3%.

    Meadowfoam Seed Oil - Derived from the seeds of the Limnanthes alba plant, which contain approximately 20-30% fat. The oil is obtained by cold pressing them. The name "Meadowfoam" comes from the appearance of the plant's flowers, which when blooming resemble foam floating in a meadow (from English: meadow - foam). Due to the huge (over 98%) content of long-chain fatty acids, it has a nourishing and moisturizing effect. Gives a light, silicone feel and absorbs quickly. Arctic cranberry seed oil – Arctic cranberries grow wild in peat bogs and humid forest areas of the Arctic tundra. Arctic cranberry seed oil has an unusually high level of tocotrienols, which are 40-60 times stronger than natural vitamin E and prevent the degradation of intercellular membranes. They protect the skin and prevent premature aging. This oil has an ideal ratio of omega 3, 6 and 9 EFAs, which are perfectly absorbed by the skin, having a moisturizing and anti-aging effect. An excellent ingredient for mature and dry skin, it has a slight sun protection effect, which additionally protects the skin against sunbathing.

    Blueberry seed oil - Organically produced, unrefined, cold pressed from the blueberry bush Vaccinium corymbosum, originating from eastern Canada and the USA. Blueberry seed oil is rich in essential fatty acids (omega-3 and omega-6, alpha linolenic acid), phospholipids, carotenoids (vitamin A) and rare ellagitannins, which can protect against free radicals and UV radiation. Intended for problematic, damaged, mature and sensitive skin, as well as acne-prone skin. It deeply moisturizes the skin, without making it excessively greasy or clogging pores, and protects and repairs the skin barrier. Helps reduce the visibility of fine wrinkles. It supports faster regeneration of the epidermis thanks to the high content of antioxidants - including phenolic compounds and tocopherols.

    Raspberry Seed Oil – Unrefined red raspberry seed oil is cold pressed from the seeds of the Rubus idaeus berry, widely grown in temperate regions of Europe and northern Asia. Raspberry seed oil has an excellent nutritional profile, contains 85% of essential fatty acids, and is a rich source of antioxidants - including both tocopherols and tocotrienols - which contribute to better fighting free radicals and protecting lipids that protect the skin against environmental stressors. Raspberry oil also offers impressive levels of Ellagitanin, considered one of the strongest antioxidants. It is also particularly rich in linolenic acid, which may help alleviate symptoms associated with inflammatory conditions such as eczema and psoriasis. Raspberry seed oil is suitable for use in a wide range of care products - from anti-aging, through anti-acne, to products dedicated to irritated or sensitive skin. It has been shown to have UV-blocking properties, soothing irritation and swelling, blemishes and symptoms of rosacea. The high level of natural vitamin E perfectly protects the skin against the effects of environmental stress, reduces wrinkles and helps maintain its natural beauty. Due to the high content of phospholipids, raspberry seed oil is very stable.

    Sunflower oil – Often underestimated and associated only with gastronomic use. Meanwhile, it is an oil perfectly tolerated by human skin. It has a very rich composition - it contains as much as 90% EFA, carotenes and vitamin E. It is well absorbed and does not leave a greasy feeling. Strengthens epidermal barriers, perfectly softens and smoothes the skin. It has anti-radical and normalizing properties. It does not cause the formation of blackheads because it has a zero comedogenic index. It is perfect for oily skin because it regulates sebum secretion. It is recommended for all skin types - including dry, mature and problematic.

    Squalane - is a raw material obtained from sugar cane, 100% natural and biodegradable. It is a more stable equivalent of human squalene (a component of our own sebum - the lipid layer that covers and protects us). Squalane is an ultra-light, odorless, hypoallergenic and perfectly absorbing oil. Perfect for all skin types, including delicate and sensitive. It moisturizes, protects and nourishes the skin without leaving it feeling heavy.

    Caprylic/ Capric Triglyceride - This 100% natural emollient is a mixture of fatty acids obtained from coconut oil esterified with glycerin. It has the ability to retain water in the skin, creating a lipid barrier on its surface. It is an excellent substance that moisturizes, smoothes and provides proper protection for the skin. It makes the skin soft, elastic and smooth.
