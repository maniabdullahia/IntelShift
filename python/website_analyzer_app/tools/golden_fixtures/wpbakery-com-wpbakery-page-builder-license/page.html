Questions / Answers

What support can I get from you?

WPBakery Page Builder comes with detailed documentation and tutorials to help you get started. If you have questions, you can open a support ticket through our online system.

Each license includes Support Plus free for 1 year. It gives you access to ticket support, auto-updates, the template library, AI, and typography features. After 1 year, you can renew Support Plus manually ($59 to extend for 1 year) or subscribe for auto-renewals and priority support ($59/year).

Do you offer discounts?

Yes, we do. For bulk license discounts please contact us at [email protected]. Please note that bulk licenses are starting from 20+ licenses.

For student discounts, please contact us at [email protected] from your Univesity-assigned email. Please note that the discount applies only to Regular license purchases.

If you’re subscribed to Support Plus, you’re automatically enrolled in our Loyalty Program. For each consecutive year your subscription stays active, your renewal price gets a 5% discount – up to a maximum of 50%.

Is there a way to test WPBakery Page Builder for free before I make a purchase?

Yes, you can try WPBakery Page Builder for free by visiting our online demo site and creating your personal demo sandbox.

Is the WPBakery license a one-time payment?

Yes, WPBakery Page Builder Regular and Theme Integration licenses are lifetime deals – which means you pay only once and get lifetime access to them.

Does WPBakery Page Builder have pre-made templates?

Yes, WPBakery Page Builder has a repository of free premium quality layout templates that you can download and use on your WordPress website.

As a theme developer - can I include WPBakery Page Builder in my theme and sell it?

Yes, you can! Please study theme integration at our knowledge base.

Does WPBakery Page Builder work with my theme?

WPBakery Page Builder works with any properly coded WordPress theme with no problems – it is designed to adjust to any theme as much as possible.

What about plugin updates?

WPBakery Page Builder is updated on a regular basis, and as an owner of the license, you will receive all updates for a lifetime completely free of charge.

Furthermore, auto-updates are available with an active Support Plus period, while manual updates are available to all license owners, even if the Support Plus period has expired.

Do you offer a loyalty program for Support Plus?

Yes. The WPBakery Loyalty Program rewards active Support Plus subscribers with a growing discount. You’ll get 5% off your renewal price for every consecutive year your subscription auto-renews – up to 50% total. The discount is applied automatically.

If your subscription is canceled or expires, the discount resets. Reactivating the subscription later will restart the discount from year one. You can manage your subscription in the Customer Center.

What is WPBakery Page Builder?

WPBakery Page Builder is a page builder plugin for WordPress which allows you to create stunning website content with simple drag and drop. With WPBakery Page Builder you can create and manage your WordPress content in minutes.
