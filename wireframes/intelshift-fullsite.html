FAQ

Common questions, answered clearly.

Everything you need to know before you start monitoring.

Is competitor website monitoring legal?+
Yes. Monitoring publicly accessible competitor web pages is legal and a standard practice in competitive intelligence. IntelShift only accesses content anyone with a browser can view — no logins, no bypassing security. We also respect robots.txt.
Do you track pages behind logins or paywalls?+
No. IntelShift only monitors publicly accessible pages such as homepages, pricing, product pages, blogs and careers pages.
How long does setup take?+
Most teams complete setup in under 5 minutes: sign up, choose your industry, add competitor URLs, choose pages, and start tracking.
Can I choose which specific pages to monitor?+
Yes. You choose exactly which page types to monitor per competitor — homepage, pricing, product, blog, careers or custom URLs.
Do I need to log in every day to get value?+
No. Alerts and reports are delivered according to your plan, so you can review key competitor moves without checking the dashboard every day.

Our Story

Competitive intelligence was broken for most teams.

We kept seeing the same pattern: someone checks a competitor's pricing page on a Thursday, screenshots it into Slack, and the thread gets buried. Two weeks later the competitor launches a new offer and sales hears about it from a prospect on a call.

The tools that existed were too complex, too expensive, or built for enterprise analyst teams — not the founder running a 10‑person SaaS or the marketing lead managing a busy ecommerce brand.

So we built IntelShift. A system that monitors the right pages, removes the noise, scores what matters, and delivers intelligence your team can actually act on — without anyone having to remember to check.
